# Higgsfield Seedance 2.0 — Video Generation Skill Collection

A collection of 15 specialized video generation prompt skills for Seedance 2.0 on Higgsfield.

## Available Sub-Skills

| Style | Trigger | Use for |
|-------|---------|---------|
| Cinematic | `/seedance-cinematic` | Film-style, dramatic lighting, Hollywood aesthetic |
| 3D CGI | `/seedance-3d-cgi` | Computer-generated imagery, product renders |
| Cartoon | `/seedance-cartoon` | Animated cartoon style videos |
| Comic to Video | `/seedance-comic` | Comic book panel animations |
| Fight Scenes | `/seedance-fight` | Action choreography sequences |
| Motion Design Ad | `/seedance-motion` | Marketing motion graphics |
| E-commerce Ad | `/seedance-ecommerce` | Product showcase videos |
| Anime Action | `/seedance-anime` | Japanese animation style |
| Product 360° | `/seedance-360` | Full product rotation views |
| Music Video | `/seedance-music` | Music video aesthetic |
| Social Hook | `/seedance-social` | Viral social media hooks |
| Brand Story | `/seedance-brand` | Brand narrative videos |
| Fashion Lookbook | `/seedance-fashion` | Fashion/clothing showcases |
| Food & Beverage | `/seedance-food` | Food styling videos |
| Real Estate | `/seedance-realestate` | Property showcase tours |

## Usage

Load the specific sub-skill for the video style needed:
```
/seedance-cinematic — for cinematic film-like videos
/seedance-ecommerce — for product ads (most useful for marketplace)
/seedance-360       — for product 360° views
```
